About

Where discipline, precision and endurance meet.

The Modern Pentathlon Federation of Sri Lanka serves as the national authority for the sport, bringing together athletes, coaches, officials, and supporters around a shared vision of excellence and national advancement.

Through structured participation, competitive events, and sustained development, MPFSL supports the sport at every level, from grassroots growth to elite representation.
